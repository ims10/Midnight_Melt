THE STORY BEHIND THE SWEETNESS

Small beginnings.
Sweet ambitions.

Midnight Melt is a home-based dessert startup in Pune, created from a love for indulgent desserts, thoughtful presentation and the simple happiness of sharing something sweet.

Our first menu celebrates two flavours: the timeless coffee-and-cocoa character of Classic Tiramisu, and the caramelised biscuit twist of Biscoff Tiramisu.

Our goal is to make every order feel special — from the first look at the layers to the very last spoonful.
